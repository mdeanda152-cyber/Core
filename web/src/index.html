How it works

The method, in plain terms

A company spent millions on a planning platform. Two years later it is doing maybe a third of what it was bought to do. This tool measures that gap, prices it, and says what to fix first — with every formula shown, because a firm with no track record has nothing to trade on except being checkable.

Five steps, in order

The pipeline

QualifyShould we take this job at all? Go, conditional, or walk away — with reasons

Three rules the tool enforces

Why trust it

Missing data is reported, never guessed

A model without its inputs shows up as “not assessed — requires X”. Inventing a plausible number is how a young firm loses the only thing it is selling.

Gross is not recoverable

Every finding shows the size of the problem and, separately, the slice a twelve-week sprint can actually take back. Only the second is ever quoted.

Sequencing is enforced

You cannot activate optimization on top of a forecast nobody trusts. The tool blocks it and says what has to come first.

What the capture rate means

Scoring

Each capability carries a value weight of 1–5. Usage scores at 0 when licensed and untouched, 0.10 when configured but idle, 0.50 when partly used, 1.00 when the business actually runs on it. Capture is the weighted average across everything they are licensed for — capability they never bought is excluded, because nobody is accountable for that.
