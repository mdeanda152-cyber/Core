Privacy policy

Short, because we collect very little. This site runs no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What we collect

When you order
Name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address, and what you bought. This comes to us from Stripe after you pay, so that we can pack and ship the box and contact you if something goes wrong.
When you join the waitlist
Your email address, and which page you submitted it from.
When you email us
Whatever you send, kept so we can answer and to have a record if there is a later dispute about an order.

What we never receive

Your card details. Payment happens on Stripe's own checkout page. Card numbers, expiry dates and security codes never pass through this website or any system we operate — that is deliberate, and it is why checkout redirects rather than taking payment inline.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or session-recording scripts. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Two things are stored locally in your own browser and never transmitted to us:

  • guiltless-cart-v1 — the contents of your cart, so it survives a page reload. Cleared once an order completes.
  • guiltless-theme — whether you chose the light or dark theme.

Clearing your browser storage removes both. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page, governed by Stripe's privacy policy.

Who processes your data

  • Stripe — payment processing, and the source of your order and shipping details.
  • Our hosting and delivery providers — serve these pages and run the checkout endpoint. They process IP addresses in ordinary server logs for security and abuse prevention.
  • Our shipping carrier — receives the name and address needed to deliver the box.
  • [EMAIL PROVIDER] — sends dispatch notices and waitlist email.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We have never done so.

How long we keep it

Order records are retained for [N] years to meet tax and accounting obligations. Waitlist addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Support email is kept for two years.

Your rights

Email hello@guiltless.example to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to unsubscribe. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of — but the access and deletion rights apply and are exercised through the same address. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Pages and the checkout endpoint are served over HTTPS. Payment credentials are handled entirely by Stripe, a PCI Service Provider Level 1. Order data reaching us is limited to what is needed to ship a box.

Changes

If we change this policy materially we will update the date below, and notify waitlist subscribers by email before the change takes effect.
