Shipping, returns & refunds

Ice cream that arrives soft has already failed. This page says exactly how we ship it, and exactly what we do when that goes wrong.

Where we ship

The contiguous United States only. We do not currently ship to Alaska, Hawaii, PO boxes, APO/FPO addresses, or internationally — a 48-hour frozen transit is the limit of what a dry-ice box reliably holds, and those lanes run longer than that.

What it costs

Shipping
$12.99 flat, or free when your order subtotal reaches $75 after any volume discount.
Minimum order
4 pints. This is physics rather than an upsell — a dry-ice box needs enough frozen mass to hold temperature for two days, and below four pints the thermal load is too small.

When it ships

Orders are packed and dispatched Monday through Wednesday, so nothing sits in a depot over a weekend. An order placed Wednesday afternoon ships the following Monday.

Transit is two business days. You will receive a dispatch email with tracking when the box actually leaves — not when the order is placed.

When it arrives

Someone does not need to be home, but the box should get into a freezer within a few hours of delivery. Dry ice sublimates continuously; the pack is rated for roughly 48 hours from dispatch, and a box left on a porch on a hot afternoon is spending that margin fast.

There will be dry ice remaining in the liner. Handle it with a towel rather than bare hands, and let any remaining pellets sublimate in a ventilated room — never in a sealed container or a closed car.

If it arrives soft, melted or damaged

Email hello@guiltless.example within 48 hours of delivery with your order reference and a photo of the box and the pints.

We will send a replacement or refund that order in full, your choice. We will not ask you to ship anything back — returning melted ice cream helps nobody, and we would rather spend that money on getting the next box right.

Returns

We cannot accept returns of ice cream that has left our cold chain, for the same food-safety reasons no grocer will take back a thawed pint. This does not affect the replacement or refund above, and it does not affect any right you have under applicable consumer law.

Cancelling or changing an order

Email us before your dispatch notice and we will cancel and refund in full, or change the flavours. Once a box is packed with dry ice it is committed and we cannot recall it.

Wrong or undeliverable address

Check your shipping address carefully at checkout. If a box is undeliverable because of an incorrect address, or is refused at delivery, we cannot re-ship it — it will have thawed. We will refund the merchandise minus shipping in that case, and we would much rather catch it before dispatch, so tell us immediately if you spot a mistake.

Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Stripe releases them immediately on our end; your bank typically posts them within 5–10 business days.

Delays outside our control

Carrier delays, severe weather and heat events do happen. If a delay puts a box outside its frozen window we treat it exactly like a melted arrival above — replacement or refund, no return. You are not out of pocket because a truck was late.
