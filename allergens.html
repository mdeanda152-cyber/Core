Allergens & nutrition

Everything here is stated plainly rather than buried. If you have an allergy or a sensitive gut, this is the page that matters.

Every flavour contains milk

Guiltless is a dairy product. All eight flavours contain milk — milk protein isolate, micellar casein, whey protein, dairy cream and butterfat. There is no dairy-free version and we are not close to one; the entire texture system is built on casein micelles.

Fibre and allulose — read this if your gut is sensitive

A pint contains roughly 18 g of soluble fibre and 18 g of allulose. That is a lot of both, and it is the honest cost of the macros.

For most people a serving is comfortable and a whole pint in one sitting is fine. For some people it is not. Allulose is generally well tolerated below about 30–40 g in a sitting, and a full pint sits at 18 g — but it stacks with whatever else is in your day, and soluble fibre at this level can cause bloating or loosening in people who are not used to it.

If you are sensitive, or you follow a low-FODMAP protocol, start with half a pint and see how you get on. We would rather tell you this here than have you find out afterwards.

Not suitable for

  • Anyone with a milk allergy — every flavour, without exception.
  • Children under 12 months, as with any product containing this concentration of protein and fibre.
  • Anyone advised to limit protein intake for kidney or liver reasons. A pint carries 42 g; check with your clinician.

Guiltless contains no aspartame, so the phenylketonuric warning does not apply. It contains no erythritol or other sugar alcohols.

How our nutrition figures are derived

Every calorie and macro figure published on this site is calculated from ingredient specifications using standard Atwater factors, plus the FDA's permitted 0.4 kcal/g for allulose. They are formulation targets.

They are not laboratory-verified, and a printed Nutrition Facts panel legally requires third-party analytical testing. Until that testing is complete, treat these as accurate intent rather than a certified label. We publish the full derivation on the formulation page, including what we are unsure about.

Health claims we are not making

Guiltless is a dessert with a favourable macro profile. It is not a medical food, a meal replacement, or a weight-loss product, and nothing on this site should be read as a claim to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
