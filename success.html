Order confirmed  ·  packed frozen  ·  2-day

That's yours. It ships frozen.

A receipt is on its way to the address you gave Stripe. We pack on dry ice and dispatch Monday to Wednesday, so nothing sits in a depot over a weekend.

What happens next

You'll get two emails

A payment receipt from Stripe immediately, then a dispatch note with tracking when the box actually leaves. If the second one hasn't arrived within three business days, reply to the first and we'll chase it.

When it lands

Freezer within the hour

There will be dry ice in the liner — handle it with a towel, not bare hands, and let any remaining pellets sublimate in a ventilated room rather than a sealed bin. The pints go straight to the freezer.

If it arrives soft

We replace it, no return

Send a photo of the box and the pints. We'll ship a replacement — we won't ask you to send anything back, because returning melted ice cream helps nobody.

Give it a few minutes before scooping

Pints arrive colder than a home freezer — around −25 °C rather than −18 °C. The allulose does its job at normal freezer temperature, so after a few hours in yours it will scoop straight out. Two or three minutes on the counter is plenty; you should never need a microwave.
