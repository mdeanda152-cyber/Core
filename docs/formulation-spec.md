> binding (~3.7 g water per g casein) closes much of the functional gap but not all of it.
> This is the largest open texture problem in v1.0. More fibre is *not* the answer — §5 is
> already at the tolerance ceiling.

## 4. Freezing-point balance

Scoopability is set by dissolved *molar* concentration. Relative FPD factor is referenced
to sucrose (MW 342) = 1.0.

| Solute | g / pint | MW | Factor | Sucrose-equiv |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| D-allulose | 18.0 | 180 | 1.9 | 34.2 |
| Milk salts & minerals | ~2.0 | varies | ~4.0 | 8.0 |
| Lactose | 7.3 | 342 | 1.0 | 7.3 |
| Sea salt (dissociates) | 0.6 | 58.5 | ~11 | 6.6 |
| Soluble fibre blend | 17.9 | ~1,500+ | ~0.35 | 6.3 |
| Sucrose | 6.0 | 342 | 1.0 | 6.0 |
| **Total** | | | | **68.4** |

**68.4 g sucrose-equivalent ÷ 253 g water = 27.0 g per 100 g water.**
Premium reference window: 26–30. We sit at the firm end — soft enough to scoop from a
domestic freezer, firm enough to hold shape on a cone.

### Rejected v0.9 formulation

Allulose 26 g + glycerol 3 g → **40 g / 100 g water**. Gummy, slumping, fast-melting.
Fix: drop glycerol entirely, cut allulose by 8 g, spend the recovered ~16 kcal on butterfat.
This trade is the reason the priority stack ranks texture above macros.

| Lactose | 7.3 | 0.16× | 1.2 |
| **Total** | | | **51.6 → 14.4%** |

**Tolerance ceilings (do not exceed without new testing):**
- Allulose ≤ 20 g/pint — GI comfort above ~30–40 g in a sitting
- Fibre ≤ 18 g/pint — at ceiling now
- Casein ≤ 88% of total protein — pasty above this
- Base pH ≥ 6.4 — casein destabilises toward pI 4.6

## 6. Process spec

| Step | Parameter | Failure mode if wrong |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Dry blend | All powders premixed, allulose as carrier | Gum lumps with hydrated skin, dry core → grit |
| 2. Hydrate | High shear, 4 °C | Under-hydrated protein → sandy |
| 3. Pasteurise | **72–74 °C / 15–20 s HTST** | Batch hold at 82 °C denatures whey → chalk |
| 4. Homogenise | **2,000 / 500 psi, two-stage, 65–70 °C** | Single-stage leaves clusters → greasy |
| 5. Age | **8–12 h @ 2–4 °C** | Fat stays liquid → no partial coalescence, ever |
| 6. Freeze | Continuous, draw **−6 to −7 °C**, 45% overrun | Slow freeze → crystals > 50 µm → icy |
| 7. Harden | **Core −30 °C within 90 min** | Slow hardening undoes step 6 entirely |
| 8. Store / serve | Store −25 °C · temper to −13 °C | Freeze–thaw cycling drives recrystallisation |

**Yolk-containing bases** (flagship, salted caramel) take a gentler, longer pasteurisation
hold in place of step 3 — coagulation risk outweighs the whey-aggregation risk at 0.5% yolk.

### QC gates per batch
- Mix viscosity post-ageing
- Draw temperature and overrun logged
- Mean ice crystal size by cryo-microscopy — **target < 25 µm** (creaminess threshold; > 50 µm reads icy)
- Hardness by penetrometer at −13 °C
- Melt rate by drip test
- Heat-shock survival: 6 accelerated freeze–thaw cycles, re-measure crystal size

pressure and low fat reduces retention/release — a dose calibrated on a warm fatty base
disappears at −13 °C and 2.8% fat.

## 8. Open problems (v1.0)

1. **Solids-short at 29.5%.** Largest remaining texture gap. Investigating higher-MW starch
   hydrolysate to add solids without FPD or fibre load.
2. **Fibre + allulose stack.** 18 g + 18 g per pint is fine for most people eating a whole
   pint, not for everyone. Label plainly; do not bury.
3. **Acid flavours blocked.** pH ≥ 6.4 constraint caps real fruit acid. Bright citrus and
   passionfruit are not solved at this protein load.
4. **Retail cold chain.** Every claim here is easiest to hit behind a counter. Retail
   distribution abuse is a different problem and gates Phase 3.
5. **Allulose supply concentration.** Most expensive functional ingredient, narrow supply
   base, no functional substitute. Carried deliberately as a business risk.

## 9. Change control

Formulations are versioned. A change ships only if it wins or ties on **every** axis of the
sensory lexicon (iciness, chalkiness, cooling burn, melt rate, flavour intensity, aftertaste)
and improves at least one. Log the reason and the sensory delta. Keep rollback available.

| Version | Date | Change | Sensory delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| v0.9 | — | Initial: allulose 26 g + glycerol 3 g | Rejected — gummy, slumping, fast melt |
| v1.0 | — | Glycerol removed, allulose → 18 g, fat → 10 g | Firm scoop, slower melt, richer palate |
