Why it costs more and where it goes

A premium pint, priced like one

Nine fifty is above the category. Here is exactly what the extra buys, because "premium" on its own means nothing.

Ingredient deck

Allulose, Reb M, real butterfat

Allulose is the most expensive functional ingredient in the formula and there is no substitute for what it does. Reb M costs several times what ordinary stevia does, and it is the reason the base does not taste artificially sweetened.

Density

359 g in the pint, not 256

We run 45% overrun against Halo Top's ~82%. You are buying roughly 25% more actual ice cream per pint than a high-overrun competitor — air is the cheapest ingredient there is, and we use less of it.

Cold chain

Dry ice, two-day, insulated

Ice cream that arrives soft has already lost. Shipping frozen costs real money — it is why the pack minimum is four pints, and why we would rather charge for it honestly than hide it in the unit price.

Questions the ones that matter

Before you order

How does it ship, and will it arrive frozen?

Packed on dry ice in an insulated liner and sent two-day. Orders leave Monday to Wednesday so nothing sits in a depot over a weekend. If a pack arrives soft, we replace it — send a photo of the box and we will not ask you to ship anything back.

Why is there a four-pint minimum?

Physics, not upselling. A dry-ice box needs enough frozen mass to hold temperature for 48 hours; below about four pints the thermal load is too small and the product softens in transit. We would rather set a minimum than ship you something that arrives wrong.

Will 18 g of fibre and 18 g of allulose upset my stomach?

For most people, a serving is fine and a whole pint in one sitting is fine. For some people it is not, and it stacks with whatever else is in your day. Allulose is generally well tolerated below roughly 30–40 g in a sitting; a full pint has 18 g. We would rather say this plainly than bury it — if you are sensitive to fibre or sugar alcohols, start with half a pint.

Does it scoop straight from the freezer?

Yes — that is the whole point of the allulose. No microwaving, no twenty minutes on the counter. Give it two or three minutes if your freezer runs very cold. Move the sliders on the home page to see exactly why.

What is in it, exactly?

All 24 ingredients are published with the reason each one is there, including the ones most brands would not list. Read the ingredient deck. No erythritol, and six grams of real sugar we kept on purpose.

Can I subscribe?

Not yet. We would rather get the cold chain right on one-off orders before committing to a recurring shipment we might miss. Join the list and we will tell you when it opens.
